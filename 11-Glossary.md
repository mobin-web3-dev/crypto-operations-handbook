# Crypto Operations Glossary

## Introduction

This glossary contains common cryptocurrency and blockchain terms that are frequently used by Operations, Customer Support, Risk, and Compliance teams.

---

## Address

A unique identifier used to send or receive cryptocurrency on a blockchain network.

---

## AML (Anti-Money Laundering)

Policies and procedures designed to prevent financial crimes such as money laundering and terrorist financing.

---

## Blockchain

A distributed digital ledger that records transactions across multiple computers in a secure and transparent manner.

---

## Block Confirmation

The process of verifying that a transaction has been included in a blockchain block. Most platforms require multiple confirmations before crediting deposits.

---

## Cold Wallet

A cryptocurrency wallet that remains offline, providing a higher level of security for long-term asset storage.

---

## DeFi (Decentralized Finance)

Financial applications that operate on blockchain networks without relying on traditional financial institutions.

---

## Deposit

The process of transferring cryptocurrency into a platform or wallet.

---

## Gas Fee

The fee required to process transactions on certain blockchain networks, especially Ethereum and compatible chains.

---

## Hot Wallet

A wallet connected to the internet, commonly used for daily transactions and operational liquidity.

---

## KYC (Know Your Customer)

The identity verification process used by financial institutions and crypto platforms to comply with regulations.

---

## Layer 1

A primary blockchain network such as Bitcoin, Ethereum, Solana, or BNB Chain.

---

## Layer 2

A secondary protocol built on top of a Layer 1 blockchain to improve scalability, reduce fees, or increase transaction speed.

---

## Memo / Destination Tag

An additional identifier required by certain cryptocurrencies to correctly route deposits to individual user accounts.

---

## Multi-Signature (Multi-Sig)

A wallet configuration that requires approval from multiple private keys before a transaction can be executed.

---

## Node

A computer that participates in maintaining and validating a blockchain network.

---

## Private Key

A confidential cryptographic key that provides complete control over cryptocurrency assets.

---

## Public Key

A cryptographic key used to generate wallet addresses and receive cryptocurrency.

---

## Recovery Phrase (Seed Phrase)

A sequence of words used to recover access to a cryptocurrency wallet. It must always remain private.

---

## Smart Contract

Self-executing software deployed on a blockchain that automatically performs predefined actions when specific conditions are met.

---

## Stablecoin

A cryptocurrency designed to maintain a stable value by being linked to another asset, such as the US Dollar.

Examples include USDT and USDC.

---

## Token

A digital asset created on an existing blockchain network.

---

## Transaction Hash (TXID)

A unique identifier assigned to every blockchain transaction.

Operations teams use the TXID to verify transaction status, confirmations, and investigate deposit or withdrawal issues.

---

## Wallet

Software or hardware used to store cryptocurrency and interact with blockchain networks.

---

## Withdrawal

The process of transferring cryptocurrency from a platform to an external wallet.

---

## Real-world Operations Notes

Operations professionals use these terms every day while investigating transactions, assisting customers, coordinating with compliance teams, and monitoring blockchain activity.

A strong understanding of this terminology improves communication, troubleshooting, and operational efficiency across crypto platforms.
